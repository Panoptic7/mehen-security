Switching from Windows? Start here.

Moving to Linux is a great step for your privacy and freedom, but it can feel unfamiliar. Mehen keeps one thing feeling just like home: your antivirus.

Familiar by design

A dashboard, Quick and Full scans, quarantine and a tray icon. If you've used antivirus on Windows, you already know how to use Mehen.

No terminal required

Install with a double-click and control everything from buttons and menus. You'll never need to type a command.

Looks after itself

Set it up once and Mehen works quietly in the background. It keeps its virus definitions up to date, runs scheduled scans and only speaks up when something needs your attention.

Shares Linux's values

Like Linux itself, Mehen is private, secure and open source. No accounts, no ads and no data leaving your computer.

Why Mehen?

Named after the coiled serpent of Egyptian myth who guarded the sun god on his journey through the night, Mehen is built to quietly guard your Linux desktop.

ClamAV, made beautiful

ClamAV is a trusted, widely used open-source antivirus engine, but on its own it's a command-line tool built for experts. Mehen wraps it in a clean, easy-on-the-eye interface, so anyone can scan, review threats and stay up to date with a few clicks.

Made for Mint & Ubuntu

Designed and tested on Linux Mint and Ubuntu, so it installs cleanly and feels at home on your desktop.

Fully open source

Every line of code is public. Read it, audit it, improve it — nothing is hidden.

Free, forever

No trials, no upsells, no subscriptions and no data collection. Just protection.

Stays up to date, on its own

You won't have to remember to update Mehen. It keeps itself current in two ways, both completely automatic.

Virus definitions, every day

Mehen regularly downloads the latest virus signatures from ClamAV's official, verified update service in the background. Your protection keeps pace with new threats, and the dashboard always shows when it last updated.

App updates in your Update Manager

New versions of Mehen appear in the normal Update Manager on Linux Mint and Software Updater on Ubuntu, right alongside your other system updates. Click Install Updates as usual, just like Windows Update, and you're on the latest version.

Signed and verified

Updates are cryptographically signed and checked before they're installed, so you only ever receive genuine Mehen releases. No extra tools, no terminal, nothing to manage.

Install

Three steps, no terminal needed. It works just like installing a program on Windows.

Download the installer

Click the Download button at the top of this page. You can also find every version on the Releases page.

Double-click to install

Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file. Your system's installer opens, just like an installer on Windows. Click Install and enter your password when asked.

Open Mehen and you're set

Find Mehen Security in your applications menu (where Windows has the Start menu). A short setup guides you through your protection choices, then Mehen takes care of the rest.

Prefer the terminal? (optional, for experienced users)

From your Downloads folder:

sudo apt install ./mehen-security_*.deb

Questions

Short answers to common ones.

I've just switched from Windows. Is Mehen right for me?

Yes. That's exactly who Mehen is designed for. It's built to feel like the Windows antivirus you know, needs no technical knowledge, and protects your computer automatically so you can focus on getting comfortable with Linux.

How is Mehen different from just using ClamAV?

ClamAV provides the detection engine, a proven and trusted layer used around the world. On its own, though, it's operated by typing commands in a terminal. Mehen adds a friendly graphical app on top of it: clear buttons, a simple dashboard and plain-English results, so everyday computer users get ClamAV's protection without any technical know-how.

Is it really free?

Yes. Mehen Security is free and open source. There is no paid version.

Which systems does it support?

Linux Mint and Ubuntu. Other Debian-based distributions may work but aren't officially tested yet.

Does Linux even need antivirus?

Linux is less targeted than other systems, but it isn't immune — and scanning helps catch malicious files before you pass them on to Windows or Mac users.

Who's behind Mehen?

Mehen is a hobby project, not a company. It started as a side project by Panoptic7, someone with a long-standing interest in network and cyber security, and grew into a real program they felt was good enough to share with the world. It isn't a commercial product and never will be. There's nothing to buy, no business model and no profit motive, just a tool built out of curiosity and given away freely in the hope it helps people feel safer on Linux.

Was AI used to build Mehen?

Yes, and we think you should know. With limited spare time and a goal of making Mehen as solid as possible, Panoptic7 uses Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to help write, check and test the code. Panoptic7 designs and directs the project and decides what goes into it. Every change is tested and published in the open. Before any stable release, Mehen's security-critical parts are planned for independent human review. If you spot something that doesn't look right, please open an issue. Scrutiny makes open source better.

How can I report a bug or help out?

Open an issue on the GitHub issues page. Contributions are welcome.
